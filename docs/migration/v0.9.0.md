# Migrating to v0.9.0

## Signal control: pedestrian detectors

Pedestrian detectors are no longer vehicle detectors with a pedestrian
type. They have their own table, `pedestrian-detectors/pedestrian-detector`,
beside `detectors/detector`.

- **Key.** `pedestrian-detector-id`, 1..255. Pedestrian detectors are
  numbered independently of vehicle detectors, so pedestrian detector 1 and
  vehicle detector 1 are different devices.
- **Config.** `name`, `enabled`, `type` (`ped-detector-pushbutton` or
  `ped-detector-passive`), a mandatory `call-phase` (the single phase the
  detector places its call on; it must resolve to an existing
  `phases/phase`), and `diagnostic-thresholds` with `no-activity-min`,
  `max-presence-min` and `erratic-counts-per-minute`.
- **State.** Mirrors the config, plus `active`, `last-activation`,
  `actuation-count` and `fault`.
- **Vehicle detectors.** The `detector-type` identities
  `detector-ped-pushbutton` and `detector-ped-passive` are removed. A vehicle
  detector no longer carries pedestrian types, and the pedestrian table has
  none of the vehicle-only leaves (such as `delay-s`, `extend-s`,
  `extend-phases` and the measurement).
- **Diagnostics.** Both tables take their thresholds from one shared
  `detector-diagnostics` grouping. The vehicle container is still
  `diagnostics`; the pedestrian one is `diagnostic-thresholds`. The vehicle
  diagnostics wire is unchanged apart from the unit-suffixed names below.

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| `detectors/detector` with `type` = `detector-ped-pushbutton` | `pedestrian-detectors/pedestrian-detector` with `type` = `ped-detector-pushbutton` |
| `detectors/detector` with `type` = `detector-ped-passive` | `pedestrian-detectors/pedestrian-detector` with `type` = `ped-detector-passive` |
| `assigned-phases` (list) | `call-phase` (single, mandatory) |

Move each pedestrian entry out of `detectors/detector`, give it a
`pedestrian-detector-id`, and set its `call-phase`.

## Detector events

Detector events name the detector by its number in its own table.

| Event | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| `detector-transition` | `channel` | `detector-id` (vehicle detector number, mandatory) |
| `pedestrian-event` | `detector-channel` | `pedestrian-detector-id` (pedestrian detector number, optional) |

Protobuf consumers: each renamed field moves to a NEW field number and the
old number and name are reserved. v0.9.0 decoders treat the old field in
stored or in-flight bytes as unknown, so re-encode or translate stored data.
The event moves:

- `DetectorTransition`: `channel` (2) to `detector_id` (11).
- `PedestrianEvent`: `detector_channel` (3) to `pedestrian_detector_id` (9).

## Unit-suffixed names

Every leaf that holds a duration, interval, delay or percentage now ends in
its unit: `-s` for seconds, `-min` for minutes, `-pct` for percent. Only the
leaf name changes; types, units and values are unchanged, and enum members
and identities that share a word with a leaf (for example the
`trailing-green` enum value) keep their names. The `tsp-event`
`adjustment-s` leaf also gained its missing `units "seconds"`.

Protobuf consumers: as in "Detector events", every renamed field moves to a
new field number and the old number and name are reserved, so stored or
in-flight bytes carry the old fields as unknown. Re-encode or translate
stored data. The regenerated `.proto` files under `api/proto/openits/` list
each reservation. The event moves:

- `DetectorReportDetector`: `occupancy` (4) to `occupancy_pct` (5).
- `TsamRecommendationApplied`: `magnitude_seconds` (4) to `magnitude_s` (12).
- `TspEvent`: `adjustment_seconds` (5) to `adjustment_s` (12).

### Core phase timing (shared by signal control and ramp metering)

The `openits-nema:phase-timing` grouping is composed by signal control
(`phases/phase` timing). Ramp metering composes its vehicle subset,
`vehicle-phase-timing` (all but `walk` and `ped-clear`), for the metering
signal timing, so its leaves are renamed too. The signal-control MUTCD
deviation modules target the renamed paths.

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| `min-green` | `min-green-s` |
| `max-green` | `max-green-s` |
| `passage` | `passage-s` |
| `yellow-change` | `yellow-change-s` |
| `red-clear` | `red-clear-s` |
| `walk` | `walk-s` |
| `ped-clear` | `ped-clear-s` |
